# Equipment research

Verified 15 September 2026 against manufacturer pages. This website sells a printing service; equipment is shown to explain the production process.

## Bambu Lab X2D

- Dual nozzles allow a separate support material where the model and material pairing are suitable.
- Main-nozzle build volume: 256 × 256 × 260 mm.
- Shared dual-nozzle build volume: 235.5 × 256 × 256 mm. Do not advertise the main-nozzle volume as the dual-nozzle volume.
- Maximum nozzle temperature: 300°C; actively heated chamber: up to 65°C.
- Automatic flow calibration and monitoring support consistency; these are not a guarantee of dimensional accuracy or flawless surfaces.
- Do not claim the optional Vision Encoder or an expanded multi-AMS configuration is installed.

## Bambu Lab AMS 2 Pro

- Four spool positions per unit; automatic filament changes for compatible materials.
- Active drying up to 65°C, automatic spool rotation and controlled venting.
- Material compatibility and the right drying temperature still need checking. Flexible TPU and abrasive filaments are not universally AMS-compatible.
- Do not claim 24/25 colors, simultaneous drying during every print, or complete drying of every engineering filament.

Source: https://uk.store.bambulab.com/products/ams-2-pro

## Creality Space Pi — single spool

- Original single-spool model, not Space Pi Plus, X4 or X4L.
- Adjustable drying temperature: 45–70°C.
- 360° PTC hot-air heating, real-time humidity monitoring, timer up to 48 hours.
- Customer benefit: material preparation can help reduce moisture-related surface defects; not a guarantee of perfectly dry material.

Source: https://store.creality.com/products/creality-space-pi-filament-dryer
